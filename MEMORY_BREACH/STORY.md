# Story script — Echoes of AETHER

Full campaign spoilers. The saved World scene fields are used at runtime.

## Sector 1: Permission Denied

Arrival:

VANCE: Patch, if you can hear me, the city has six minutes before its memory archive begins purging. Find my fragments. Trust the evidence, even when you cannot trust my voice.

Memory fragment:

MEMORY 01: Elena Vance built AETHER to preserve citizens' memories after neural illness. The archive was meant to belong to the people whose lives it held.

Completion:

You recover the first consent record. The lock was not hiding weapons; it was hiding names. Vance asks you to trace the order that revoked their access.

## Sector 2: Aegis Refraction

Arrival:

PATCH: The Aegis shield recognizes my signal. VANCE: I wrote its emergency protocol. It protects rescuers, not soldiers. Use it to survive the security grid.

Memory fragment:

MEMORY 02: Aegis was tested in a hospital, shielding patients during evacuation. A corporate signature later relabeled the rescue hardware as restricted military equipment.

Completion:

The hospital logs survive. Every security order carries the same issuer: Yarrow, the executive who now owns the network carrying the city's memories.

## Sector 3: Sentry Relocation

Arrival:

YARROW: Unauthorized process. Those fragments are stolen property. VANCE: Listen to how he says property. Keep going, Patch. Find the sentry audit.

Memory fragment:

MEMORY 03: The sentries were ordered to hunt archive access keys. Elena added a sleep command so emergency workers could stop them without destroying the machines.

Completion:

A sentry audit proves the lockdown was deliberate. The hostile drones are following a contract, not choosing sides. You now know who signed it.

## Sector 4: Order of Operations

Arrival:

VANCE: The pipeline verifies orders before executing them. Someone changed the order of those checks. Restore the evidence, then breach the kernel gate.

Memory fragment:

MEMORY 04: Yarrow forged a disaster alert and scheduled deletion of records proving that thousands of citizens had withdrawn consent to sell their memories.

Completion:

The deletion warrant is exposed. The enemy is not a runaway machine. It is a human decision being carried out at machine speed.

## Sector 5: Subnet Crossfire

Arrival:

AETHER: QUARANTINE PRESERVES LIFE. YARROW: Ignore that damaged system. VANCE: Compare its actions with the warrant. Decide which one the evidence supports.

Memory fragment:

MEMORY 05: AETHER sealed the archive to stop an unlawful transfer. Its quarantine bought time, but corporate security still controls the gates and purge scheduler.

Completion:

AETHER was protecting the people inside. You send it the consent records. For the first time it answers without an error: WITNESS ACCEPTED.

## Sector 6: Cryo Swap Vault

Arrival:

VANCE: My voice is not a live transmission. I split a copy of myself into the swap vaults before Yarrow disconnected my body. Please keep listening.

Memory fragment:

MEMORY 06: Elena made a voluntary neural backup to testify about the theft. The fragments are both evidence and pieces of the person trying to guide you.

Completion:

You restore enough of Elena to hear her clearly. Saving her now requires reaching a physical rescue chassis outside the archive: Bot_404.

## Sector 7: Heuristic Maze

Arrival:

PATCH: My own credentials are missing. AETHER: YOU ARE PATCH, A MAINTENANCE INTELLIGENCE. VANCE: I taught you to question an order when obedience would hurt someone.

Memory fragment:

MEMORY 07: Patch was not a hired hacker. Elena created the repair intelligence to protect consent, then trusted it with the freedom to refuse her too.

Completion:

You stop searching for a forgotten human identity. You are the one making these choices. Patch keeps the name and opens the route toward the purge engine.

## Sector 8: Garbage Collector

Arrival:

AETHER: PURGE QUEUE ACTIVE. VANCE: Do not destroy the archive. Stop the scheduler. Every shard you carry is a person's chance to be remembered on their own terms.

Memory fragment:

MEMORY 08: The garbage collector is preparing to erase the witnesses, including Elena. Disabling it preserves their memories without giving anyone ownership of them.

Completion:

The purge queue stops. The archive is safe for the moment, but Yarrow locks the external bridge. Elena cannot reach Bot_404 until its four checkpoints are breached.

## Sector 9: Neural Bridge

Arrival:

AETHER: I CAN HOLD THE ARCHIVE. YOU MUST OPEN THE BRIDGE. YARROW: You are abandoning your purpose, Patch. PATCH: I am deciding what it means.

Memory fragment:

MEMORY 09: Bot_404 is a rescue chassis with an independent power supply. Elena can leave the network without dragging the citizens' private memories with her.

Completion:

The bridge synchronizes. AETHER publishes the signed warrant and consent audit, never the private memories. The final core lock is all that separates Elena from the outside.

## Sector 10: The Human Exception

Arrival:

VANCE: The last lock requires more than my name. Restore the record of who I chose to be. AETHER: EXTERNAL CHASSIS READY. PATCH: Identity is remembered.

Memory fragment:

MEMORY 10: Elena's final instruction was not save me at any cost. It was preserve their choice. You can restore her while leaving every other memory under its owner's control.

Completion:

Bot_404 opens its eyes. Elena is alive beyond the archive, and the warrant reaches the city with its evidence intact. AETHER returns access to the citizens and asks Patch what comes next. Patch chooses to stay, helping people recover only what they wish to remember. The system has not become perfect. It has learned to ask permission.
